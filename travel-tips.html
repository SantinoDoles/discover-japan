Travel Tips for Visiting Japan

Planning a trip to Japan? Here are some helpful tips to make your visit easier, safer, and more enjoyable.

Transportation in Japan

Japan has an excellent public transportation system, including trains, subways, and buses. The Shinkansen, also known as the bullet train, allows travelers to move quickly between major cities. Visitors can also use prepaid IC cards to make paying for transportation easier.

Japanese Etiquette

Respect and politeness are important parts of Japanese culture. Visitors should remove their shoes when entering certain homes and traditional buildings. Speaking quietly on public transportation and following local customs are also appreciated.

Money and Payments

Japan uses the Japanese yen (JPY). Credit cards are widely accepted in major cities, but carrying some cash is helpful when visiting smaller shops, restaurants, and rural areas. Travelers can exchange currency or withdraw yen from compatible ATMs.

Japan Travel Checklist

  • Make sure your passport is valid before traveling.
  • Learn a few basic Japanese phrases before your trip.
  • Bring a portable charger for your phone.
  • Carry some Japanese yen for shops that don't accept cards.
